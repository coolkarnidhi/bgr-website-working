Milestones

1985 to 2026
19
  1. 85
    Founder · B. G. Raghupathy

    A science graduate and first-generation entrepreneur, Mr. B. G. Raghupathy identified opportunities in the power sector early in life, and negotiated with foreign companies to secure large contracts for GEA.

    GEA Energietechnik GmbH enters a 40:60 joint venture with him, setting up GEA Energy System India Private Limited.

  2. 86

    Lays the foundation with a small factory at Pannamgadu Industrial Estate, Nellore, beginning with fabrication work for BHEL’s ancillary unit in Ranipet.

  3. 87

    Before civil works are complete, receives its first order, worth Rs. 17 lakhs, from Neyveli Lignite Corporation. The company manufactures condenser tube cleaning systems and debris filters, both import substitutes.

  4. 9192

    Wins the Indo-German Chamber of Commerce Award for outstanding export performance, and an order from Tenaga Nasional Berhad of Malaysia as part of its modernisation programme, against a tough international competitor.

  5. 93

    GEA Energietechnik divests its stake to Mr. B. G. Raghupathy. Through 2000, he enters many technical collaborations and joint ventures for power sector equipment and services.

  6. 95

    Establishes the Air Fin Cooler (AFC) Division.

  7. 96

    Establishes the Environmental Engineering Division.

  8. 98

    Establishes the Power Projects Division.

  9. 00

    Wins its first Balance of Plant contract, at Valathur, pioneering BoP in India and creating a new market for execution outside the power island of boiler, turbine and generator.

  10. 02

    Establishes the Oil & Gas Equipment Division.

  11. 03

    Wins its first EPC contract, a 120 MW gas-based combined cycle power plant at Karupur.

  12. 06

    Wins its first Balance of Plant contract for a 500 MW unit, at the Vijayawada Thermal Power Station.

  13. 07

    Renamed BGR Energy Systems Limited on 28 June, a turnkey service provider for power plants. The IPO, open from 5 to 12 December, is subscribed about 120 times at a 75% premium.

  14. 08

    Lists on NSE and BSE, secures large EPC contracts from RRVUNL and TANGEDCO, including a 1 x 600 MW expansion at the Mettur Thermal Power Station.

  15. 10

    Signs a JV with Hitachi Power Europe for supercritical boilers and another with Hitachi Limited, Japan for steam turbines and generators, going on to win orders from NTPC and State Electricity Boards.

  16. 11

    The Electrical Projects Division wins a contract from NPCIL for work at Kota, Rajasthan, and Kakrapar, Gujarat.

  17. 12

    Wins supercritical boiler orders from NTPC, and the steam turbine and generator contract for NTPC’s Lara plant, 2 x 800 MW. Signs an MoU with the Tamil Nadu government for boiler and turbine factories at Cheyyur, Kanchipuram district, alongside Hitachi.

    Between 2000 and 2012 India adds over 60,000 MW of thermal capacity. BGR is one of the largest private-sector contributors, at roughly 25% of thermal EPC at peak, behind only BHEL.

  18. 14

    Wins the Balance of Plant contract from OPGCL for 2 x 660 MW at Ib Valley, Odisha.

  19. 16

    Wins Balance of Plant contracts for supercritical units of 1 x 800 MW, 1 x 800 MW and 3 x 660 MW. Mr. Arjun Govind Raghupathy is inducted to the Board as Deputy Managing Director and Chief Operating Officer.

  20. 20

    Mr. Arjun Govind Raghupathy is appointed Managing Director.

  21. 26

    New growth engines defined and early market-entry work underway.
